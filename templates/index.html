System Workflow

How the Posture Guardian System Works

The Posture Guardian and Spine Health Predictor follows a structured workflow to analyse body posture, detect unhealthy sitting habits, and provide corrective guidance using artificial intelligence.

Step 1: Live Camera Input

The system captures real time video using a webcam or device camera while the user is sitting or working.

Step 2: Body Keypoint Detection

Computer vision techniques detect key body points such as head, neck, shoulders, spine, and hips from each video frame.

Step 3: Posture Analysis

The detected key points are analysed to calculate body angles and alignment in order to identify correct or incorrect posture.

Step 4: Risk Level Estimation

Based on posture deviation and duration, the system calculates a posture risk level indicating potential strain on the spine.

Step 5: Alerts and Feedback

If unhealthy posture is detected, real time alerts and visual feedback are shown to help the user correct their posture immediately.

Step 6: Corrective Guidance

The system suggests simple posture correction exercises and ergonomic tips to improve spine health over time.
