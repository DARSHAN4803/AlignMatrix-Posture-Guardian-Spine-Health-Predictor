About the Project

AI-Based Posture Monitoring and Spine Health Prediction

The Posture Guardian & Spine Health Predictor is designed to monitor body posture continuously using computer vision and pose estimation techniques. The system detects improper spine alignment, neck bending, side leaning, and excessive knee flexion during daily activities such as sitting, standing, and sleeping.

Real-time posture classification provides instant feedback including posture status, detected issue, risk percentage, and corrective exercise suggestions. Additionally, posture data is periodically recorded under each user profile. By analysing repeated posture deviations over time, the system identifies dominant posture problems and generates personalised health recommendations to prevent long-term musculoskeletal disorders.

Real-Time Posture Detection and Long-Term Spine Health Monitoring
