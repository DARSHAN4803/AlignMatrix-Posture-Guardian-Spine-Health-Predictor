Methodology

System Methodology

Step-by-Step Implementation of Posture Guardian & Spine Health Predictor

The Posture Guardian system integrates computer vision, pose estimation, rule-based posture classification, and database-driven analytics to monitor, analyse, and predict long-term spine health risks.

1. Real-Time Data Capture

The system captures live video using a webcam or mobile camera. Frames are continuously processed to detect human body landmarks using MediaPipe Pose estimation.

2. Pose Estimation

Key body landmarks such as shoulders, hips, knees, and nose are extracted to construct a skeletal model. This allows measurement of spine alignment and joint positioning.

3. Angle Calculation

Mathematical computations are performed to calculate spine depth, side bending, neck inclination, and knee joint angles. These values represent deviations from ideal posture alignment.

4. Rule-Based Posture Classification

Based on predefined threshold values, posture is classified into categories such as Forward Bend, Backward Bend, Left Lean, Right Lean, and Knee Stress. Each posture is assigned a risk percentage.

5. Periodic Data Logging

Every 5 minutes, detected posture issues are stored in a MySQL database under the logged-in user ID. This creates a structured posture history for long-term analysis.

6. Long-Term Issue Analysis

Stored posture logs are analysed to determine the most frequently occurring posture issue for each user. This identifies dominant spine stress patterns over time.

7. Health Risk Prediction & Recommendations

Based on repeated posture deviations and accumulated risk levels, the system generates personalised recommendations including corrective exercises, ergonomic suggestions, and posture awareness alerts. This preventive approach helps reduce the likelihood of chronic back pain and musculoskeletal disorders.
