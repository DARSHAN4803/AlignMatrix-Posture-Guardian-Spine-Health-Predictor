Posture Guardian - Live Detection

Monitor your sitting, standing, and sleeping posture in real-time and receive instant corrective feedback.

Click the button below to begin real-time posture monitoring using your camera.

The Posture Guardian & Spine Health Predictor is an intelligent monitoring system designed to detect improper body alignment in real-time. By using advanced computer vision techniques, the system analyses body landmarks such as the spine, neck, and knees to identify posture deviations including forward bending, backward bending, side leaning, and excessive knee flexion.

The system provides immediate feedback including detected posture type, specific issue, risk percentage, and recommended corrective exercises. This helps users improve daily posture habits during sitting, standing, and sleeping activities.

Additionally, detected posture issues are recorded periodically under the user's profile. Based on repeated posture deviations over time, the system generates personalised health recommendations to reduce the risk of chronic back pain, spinal strain, and musculoskeletal disorders.

By promoting posture awareness and early correction, the Posture Guardian aims to enhance spine health and overall physical well-being.
